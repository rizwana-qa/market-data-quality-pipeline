07 · Decision support

Business intelligence observations

Monitoring priorities based on the measured historical indicators.

  1. Relative asset resilience: Monitor the BTC–ETH return spread across rolling 7-, 15- and 30-return windows. Persistent divergence is more informative than a single-session ranking.
  2. Participation confirmation: Assess price movement alongside volume, turnover and trade-count ratios. A combined increase indicates a stronger change in observed activity than volume alone.
  3. Risk normalisation: Compare ATR as a percentage of price and realised volatility before comparing the apparent risk of assets with different price levels.
  4. Session pressure: Use close location and close-versus-VWAP to describe where the session ended relative to its trading range and volume-weighted average.
  5. Co-movement and diversification: Monitor rolling correlation. High correlation in a short sample may reduce observed diversification benefits, but does not establish future correlation.
Recommended monitoring priorities

Prioritise sustained changes in relative strength, volatility, trading participation and drawdown. Escalate when multiple independent indicators confirm a material change, rather than reacting to one isolated technical measure.

08 · Data governance

Scope, methodology and limitations

Source: Supplied CSV representing Kraken spot OHLC daily records for XBT/USD and ETH/USD. The file contains 31 daily observations and 30 aligned return observations.

Return windows: Close-to-close returns. Seven-return performance compares the latest close with the close seven intervals earlier. Fifteen- and thirty-return windows follow the same convention.

RSI: 14-period simple average gains and losses. ATR: 14-period average true range. Realised volatility: sample standard deviation of daily returns annualised using √365. Alternative RSI smoothing conventions can produce slightly different readings.

Activity: Latest-session volume, estimated USD turnover and trade count are compared with the previous seven sessions. Estimated turnover uses base volume multiplied by reported VWAP. Average trade notional divides estimated turnover by recorded trade count.

Important limitations: This is an exchange-specific spot dataset, not consolidated global market data. OHLCV and trade count do not provide bid-ask spread, order-book depth, aggressor-side volume, derivatives open interest, funding rates, liquidations, ETF flows, on-chain flows, macroeconomic context or sentiment.

Analysis approach: The intelligence findings are evidence-based interpretations of calculated historical metrics. No predictive AI model or external AI signal has been run. These indicators are decision-support context, not investment advice.
